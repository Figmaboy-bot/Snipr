Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Snipr is a Chrome extension and companion web app for saving and organizing sections of web pages. This page explains what data Snipr collects, why, and how it's stored.

What we collect

  • Account info. When you sign in or sign up, we collect your email address (and, if you use "Continue with Google", the name/email your Google account shares) through Firebase Authentication. We use this only to identify your account — we never see or store your password ourselves.
  • Saved sections ("snips"). When you click a section of a page and save it, we store the section's HTML, a screenshot image, the source page URL, and any folder, category, or note you attach to it. This is the core content of the product — it's what shows up in your library.
  • Organizational metadata. The folder and category names you create, so your library looks the same across devices.

We do not collect browsing history, analytics/tracking data, or any information about pages you visit unless you actively click to save that page as a snip.

Why the extension requests broad site access

Snipr's extension requests permission to run on all sites (<all_urls>) because its core function — letting you select and save a section from any page you're browsing — only works if the content script can read that page's DOM to detect and highlight sections. The extension does not read, transmit, or store anything from a page unless you explicitly select and save a section from it.

Where data is stored

Saved snips and account data are stored in Google Firestore under your user ID. Screenshots are re-encoded as compressed JPEGs and large HTML is truncated before upload to stay under Firestore's size limits; a full-quality copy stays in your browser's local extension storage regardless of sign-in state.

Firestore security rules restrict every user's data so that only that user's signed-in account can read or write it — we do not access it, and it is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Shared links

If you use "Share collection with a link" on a folder, a read-only snapshot of that folder's snips at that moment becomes accessible to anyone with the link — no sign-in required. Only folders you explicitly choose to share are made public this way. You can revoke a share at any time from "My Shares" in the web app, which permanently deletes that snapshot.

Deleting your data

Deleting a snip from the extension or the web app removes it from the cloud. To delete your account and all associated data entirely — every saved snip, your folders/categories, and any share links you've published — sign in to the web app and click Delete Account in the header. This is immediate and permanent; it cannot be undone. Local snips stored in the extension on your own device are not affected and can be cleared from the extension's own settings.

Third-party services

Snipr is built on Firebase (authentication and database) and hosted on Vercel. Both process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security commitments; we don't share your data with any other third party.
